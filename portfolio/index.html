About me

Technical Precision Meets Web Design

I'm an Electrical & Computer Engineer who builds websites that go beyond templates. I founded DT Creative Solutions to deliver custom, high-performance web solutions to businesses across the Balkans and Germany.

Most developers use pre-built themes and call it a day. My engineering background means I understand performance, architecture and maintainability at a deeper level — so you get a site that's fast, reliable and built to last.

I work across fitness, healthcare, automotive, events, finance and more — always picking the right stack for the job: WordPress, WooCommerce, JavaScript, Python or Supabase.
