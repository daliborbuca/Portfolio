About me

Engineer turned Web Developer

I'm an Electrical & Computer Engineer with a strong passion for building web solutions that actually help businesses grow. I founded DT Creative Solutions to provide high-quality, custom websites and automation tools to clients across Serbia, Croatia and Germany.

My engineering background means I go beyond templates — I understand the technical side deeply and build things that are fast, reliable and maintainable. I've worked with clients in fitness, healthcare, real estate, events, hospitality and more.

I work with WordPress, JavaScript, Python, Supabase and custom HTML/CSS — picking the right tool for each job.
